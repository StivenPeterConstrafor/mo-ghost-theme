1 · The schools

The schools after Trent

After the Council of Trent Catholic theology was taught in schools: the Thomists of the Dominicans and the Discalced Carmelites, the Scotists of the Franciscan houses, the Jesuits after Suárez and Molina, the Augustinians of Louvain and the Sorbonne. Each stream is a school; its width is the number of its writers on this shelf at work in each decade.

2 · Where they taught

From Salamanca to the Roman College

The schools lived in universities and colleges. Suárez taught at Rome, Alcalá, Salamanca and Coimbra; Bellarmine at Louvain before Rome; Jansen came from Louvain to his see at Ypres. Scroll to follow them.

    1470

    3 · The method

    How a disputation is built

    Francisco Suárez’s Metaphysical Disputations (Salamanca, 1597) was the first complete metaphysics written as a system rather than as a commentary on Aristotle. It was read in Catholic and Protestant schools alike.

      4 · The council

      The turn at Trent

      The Council of Trent met in twenty-five sessions over eighteen years, in three periods under three popes. Its decrees on Scripture, justification and the sacraments are the ground of everything on this shelf after 1563.
