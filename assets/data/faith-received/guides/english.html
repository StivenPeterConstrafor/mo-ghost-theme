The English Divines

From Cranmer to Pusey

The English church from the break with Rome to the Oxford Movement: the Reformers and the Elizabethan bishops, the Puritans and the divines of Westminster, the Caroline and Restoration churchmen, the Dissenters after 1662, and the Methodists. Most of the shelf is the printed books of 1530 to 1700, from Early English Books.

      4 · The first printings

      As they were first printed

      880 titles on the shelf keep the spelling of their first printing beside the modern one: chaine for chain, reede for reed, Praier for prayer. Scroll to watch them change.

      Red letters are the old printer’s; underlined, the modern title’s.
